Jingwei GPS Stamp Camera

Privacy Policy

Applies to iOS app version 1.1.0.

Shuibo Liu (刘水波), the individual developer of Jingwei GPS Stamp Camera, provides the app. This policy explains how the app handles information and how you can manage it. You can use the app without a Jingwei account.

Photos, videos and settings

The app processes the photos, videos, audio, text and images you capture or select on your device. It uses your inputs to create and manage photos, videos, watermarks and QR codes. Depending on what you choose, those inputs may include a signature, logo, a child's name or birthday, or contact details you type into a QR code. The app also stores your preferences, consent choices and free-use records on the device. The app does not send this content or these records to the developer's servers.

Information printed on a watermark or encoded in a QR code, including a location, becomes part of the result. When you share a result through the iOS share sheet, the destination you select receives that result. Your iOS settings control device backups and iCloud Photos. Manage copies in Photos, backups, and other services separately.

iOS permissions

  • Camera and microphone. The camera captures photos and video. The microphone adds sound to video. You can record without sound if you decline microphone access.
  • Photos. The system photo picker provides the items you select. To save a result in Photos and create or write to an app album, the app may ask for photo library read and write access, or for permission to add photos.
  • Location. With your iOS permission, the app can read your location while the app is in use. The app requests your choice separately before using AMap's location services. You can manage iOS permissions in Settings.

Optional AMap services

The app includes AMap's iOS Maps, Location, Search and Foundation SDKs. The provider of the integrated mainland China service is Beijing AMap Tuqiang Technology Co., Ltd. (北京高德图强科技有限公司). Where those features are available, the SDKs provide maps, current location, nearby places, address lookup and local weather after you agree to the app's AMap location privacy notice. You can still use features that do not rely on AMap if you decline.

Depending on the feature, AMap receives your coordinates, the map area you view, a point you select, search terms or the city used to look up weather. If you choose a map point or search for a place, that request can be sent to AMap even when system permission for your current location is off.

Depending on the SDK, permissions and device, AMap may also process technical information such as IP address, network or positioning signals, sensor readings, app version, device model, operating system or diagnostics to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service. The bundled AMap Foundation privacy manifest also identifies user identifiers and product interactions for analytics that it declares unlinked to you and unused for tracking. The app uses the SDK version without Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request permission to track you.

AMap states that it stores information it collects in mainland China, including when its services are used outside China. AMap explains how it protects and retains information and how you can make a privacy request in its Open Platform Privacy Policy. To withdraw consent for future AMap requests in the app, open Profile, then About, then AMap Location Privacy. Withdrawal does not remove location text already saved in a photo or video.

Subscriptions

Apple handles your Apple Account, purchase payments, renewals and refunds. The app uses StoreKit on your device to verify signed app transactions and manage membership benefits. StoreKit provides information such as the product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, and renewal or revocation status. Restore Purchases asks Apple to sync your purchases. The app does not upload receipts or signed transactions to a developer server. Apple may separately provide the developer with App Store sales, subscription or refund reports. See App Store & Privacy.

Support

If you contact us through the support channel, we use the information you choose to provide, such as a description, contact information or screenshots, to respond and resolve the issue. That channel also handles messages under its own privacy rules. We retain the information we receive only as long as needed to handle the request, meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute, then delete or de-identify it. Please send only the information needed for your request.

Retention, deletion and your choices

In-app works and settings remain in your app storage until you delete them or delete the app and its data. You can delete works in the app and edit saved watermark details. The app keeps at least one profile for child milestone watermarks. You can replace its details by editing the profile. To clear all app data, save any works you want to keep before deleting the app and its data. iOS can offload the app and keep its data. Photos, external copies and backups must be managed separately. We cannot remotely view or delete content stored only on your device.

Open Profile → Contact Support in the app to ask a privacy question or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold. We may need to verify your request. For information held by Apple or AMap, use the rights and contact options in their privacy policies.

Children's information

The app is a general-purpose camera and editing tool. If you choose to include a child's information in a watermark or QR code, do so with the permission of the child's parent or guardian where required and consider who will see the result. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions about a child's information.
